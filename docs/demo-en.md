# 5–7 minute demo — Potok

**Goal:** demonstrate the connected outcome cycle, not a nominal sales pipeline: proposal → delivery → documents → cash → margin → next action. Start as **Owner / executive**, select English, and reset demo data from **Execution & profit control** if needed.

## 0:00–0:45 — Execution & profit control

1. Open **Execution & profit control**.
2. Ask: “Which actions today will improve cash flow and margin, rather than just increase the nominal pipeline value?”
3. Point out overdue receivables, actual margin, and the cash calendar.
4. Select the overdue KPI to expose its definition, source, update time, completeness, and source invoices.
5. Emphasize that manager expectations, the planned calendar, and actual cash are separate.

## 0:45–2:00 — Proposal and planned margin

1. Select **Proposal** in the top bar.
2. Choose a client, enter a title, and add a service: quantity `2`, unit price `100,000`, unit cost `40,000`.
3. Show planned revenue of `RUB 200,000`, direct costs of `RUB 80,000`, and planned margin of `RUB 120,000`.
4. Switch to RU and back mid-form. The route and input remain intact.
5. Save and approve the proposal.

## 2:00–3:00 — Contract and automatic project creation

1. Select **Create contract**; no proposal data is re-entered.
2. Mark the contract as signed. This is explicitly a demo status, not a legally binding signature.
3. Show the automatically created project, four obligations, and financial calendar.
4. Show extraction sources and confidence, then confirm amounts and deadlines as a human reviewer.

## 3:00–4:30 — Certificate, invoice, payment, and matching

1. Open the project and mark delivery as completed.
2. Create the acceptance certificate and invoice, then issue the invoice in **Money**.
3. Simulate a payment of `RUB 100,000`. The bank is a mock adapter; no real payment occurs.
4. Show that the payment starts unmatched, then select **Match payment**.
5. The invoice becomes paid and its balance and calendar event update.

## 4:30–5:45 — Actual margin and explanation

1. Add two project costs: team labor of `RUB 80,000` and rework of `RUB 30,000`.
2. Open **Margin plan vs actual**.
3. Show actual margin of `RUB 90,000` and variance of `↓ RUB 30,000`. Cash received (`RUB 100,000`) is distinct from revenue (`RUB 200,000`).
4. Prepare the controlled AI explanation and point out its source, confidence, and human review.
5. Open **Next actions** and show the margin recommendation with its reason, linked record, effect, and owner.

## 5:45–6:45 — External workflow or controlled migration

Choose one ending for the audience:

- **Client portal:** switch to **External client**, change the portal language independently, and request changes. Return to an internal role: the project is blocked and a next action exists. Costs, margin, and internal comments are not exposed.
- **Migration:** open **Test migration**, use the demo CSV, and show field mapping, duplicates, missing owners, files not transferred, the test run, and rollback.

**Closing line:** “Potok shows not just the deal value, but what to do today to deliver the promise, collect the cash, and protect margin.”
